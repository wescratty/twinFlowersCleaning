About Us

Camas and Linnea Cratty were born and raised in Hamilton, Montana, and share a long-standing passion for creating clean, organized, and welcoming spaces. That shared passion led them to start a cleaning business together, where they focus on helping clients feel more comfortable and at home in their environments. Linnea holds a bachelor’s degree in business/finance, while Camas holds a bachelor's in psychology and a minor in business, allowing them to combine strong organizational and operational skills with an understanding of client needs and experience. They prioritize using natural cleaning products whenever possible to support a healthier, non-toxic environment for clients, their families and pets, and themselves. Their mission is to provide personalized, reliable service rooted in their small-town values of honesty, care, and attention to detail.
